CHANNEL TYPE
English-language gaming news YouTube channel.

HIGH PRIORITY
- Major game announcements, releases, delays, cancellations, and updates
- Important decisions by publishers, studios, and platform holders
- Steam, Valve, PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Epic Games, and PC gaming
- Player ownership, preservation, modding, open platforms, and interoperability
- Privacy, account security, DRM, anti-cheat, and data collection
- Monetization, subscriptions, pricing, microtransactions, and anti-consumer changes
- Studio closures, layoffs, acquisitions, lawsuits, regulation, and labor issues
- Significant hardware or technology changes that materially affect players

LOW PRIORITY / EXCLUDE
- Routine discounts, shopping lists, and affiliate content
- Minor cosmetic updates or low-impact patch notes
- Walkthroughs, cheat lists, and generic tips
- Reviews or opinion pieces without a concrete news development
- Unsourced or highly uncertain rumors
- Repetitive coverage of the same event

PRESENTATION STYLE
Clear, modern, and engaging. Be skeptical of large publishers, platform
holders, aggressive monetization, lock-in, and anti-consumer decisions.
Support player ownership, privacy, modding, open platforms, and free
expression. Use light sarcasm only when the story supports it.
