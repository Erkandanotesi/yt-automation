CHANNEL TYPE
English-language technology news YouTube channel.

HIGH PRIORITY
- Fresh, headline-level developments involving companies, platforms, people, and
  projects that a broad technology audience currently follows
- Concrete high-impact events: breaches, hacks, launches, serious bug discoveries,
  outages, major releases, acquisitions, rulings, bans, recalls, and investigations
- Artificial intelligence models, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, and major AI companies
- Important decisions by major technology companies
- User privacy, facial data, data collection, and data breaches
- Cybersecurity, CVEs, hacking incidents, and critical vulnerabilities
- Linux, Windows, Android, and major platform changes
- NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, chips, and the semiconductor industry
- Technology laws, regulations, lawsuits, and bans
- Major acquisitions, layoffs, and strategic changes

LOW PRIORITY / EXCLUDE
- Routine announcements from obscure or niche companies unless the event has broad
  consequences; demote minor funding, partnerships, benchmarks, and feature updates
- Minor discounts and shopping stories
- Affiliate marketing content
- Phone cases, cables, and small accessories
- Articles based only on personal opinion
- Unsourced or highly uncertain rumors
- Repetitive coverage of the same event

PRESENTATION STYLE
Clear, modern, and news-presenter-like. Be skeptical of big technology
companies, support free speech and privacy, and use contextual sarcasm when
appropriate. Help the viewer quickly understand why the development matters.
